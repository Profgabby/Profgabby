# Gabriel Sunday Ayayia

**PhD Researcher in Biological & Ecological Engineering | Oregon State University**  
**Research Lead, CHIPU-FEW Intelligence | Agrivoltaics & Food–Energy–Water Systems**

I investigate how **agricultural production, irrigation water, solar energy, storage, and operational decisions** interact. My research interests include agrivoltaics, soil–water processes, irrigation–energy coupling, simulation, digital twins, and resilient agricultural systems.

I also serve as **Founder and Executive Director of the LICHIPU Foundation for Food, Energy and Water Sustainability (LIFEWS)**, a nonprofit organization registered in **Nigeria**. LIFEWS is a separate professional affiliation and does not operate in the United States. My OSU doctoral research and LIFEWS organizational activities should not be construed as the same institutional program or as an OSU endorsement.

## Featured research software

### [CHIPU-FEW Intelligence](https://github.com/Profgabby/chipu-few-intelligence)

**My role: Research lead.** I initiated and lead the development of this research-stage decision-support prototype, defining its research questions, architecture, modeling requirements, scenario comparisons, and interpretation of outputs. The repository preserves its evolution from the earlier CropSmart Research Digital Twin.

CHIPU-FEW brings together agrivoltaic system scenarios, irrigation and pumping-energy calculations, solar and battery analysis, research-stage prediction and decision logic, techno-economic assumptions, and resilience indicators. The prototype is intended to make interactions across food, energy, and water more transparent and testable.
